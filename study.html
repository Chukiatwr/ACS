Study

The academic journey from computing foundations to systems, AI/data, digital business, industrial training and final-year projects.

4-year learning journey

Year 1 · Foundations

Programming, discrete mathematics, calculus, linear algebra, data structures, programming languages and computer architecture.

Year 2 · Build systems

Databases, operating systems, OOP, web programming, AI/ML, statistics and an early capstone project.

Year 3 · Integrate & apply

Network computing, software engineering, data science and data engineering, electives, seminar and industrial training.

Year 4 · Specialize & demonstrate

Project proposal, advanced electives, Project Study and optional WiL pathway through CSS 495.
